# Session 8 — Capstone + Demo Day

**Duration**: 90 minutes
**Deliverable**: live demo of your project to Federico (and possibly Jana + friends)
**Meta-lesson**: shipping > perfecting.

---

## This session is different

The first 7 were you learning. This is you **showing what you learned**.

It's not a test. It's demo day. It's the part of the process where you celebrate the journey.

---

## Before you start

- [ ] Your project LIVE with public URL
- [ ] CLAUDE.md written
- [ ] At least 5 journal entries from previous sessions
- [ ] You showered and combed your hair (it's demo day, gotta look ready)

---

## Part 1 (30 min) — Prepare the demo

You'll present for 10-12 minutes what you built. Structure:

### Mental slide 1 (1 min) — What you built
- "I built [project name]. It's [one sentence]. It serves [one sentence]."

### Mental slide 2 (2 min) — Why
- Why you picked this project
- For whom

### Mental slide 3 (4 min) — Live demo
- Open the URL on screen
- Walk through the main use flow
- Show 2-3 features
- **If something breaks, don't panic**. That happens to Apple in their keynotes. Continue.

### Mental slide 4 (2 min) — What I learned
- 3 technical things
- 1 thing about yourself

### Mental slide 5 (2 min) — What's next
- What you'd add
- What project you want to do next

### Mental slide 6 (1 min) — Closing
- Thank Federico (not exaggerated — sincerely)
- Share your URL + GitHub

**Practice once fully before presenting live.** Ask Claude to listen and give feedback:

```
I'm going to present you my final demo as if you were Federico. Then tell me which parts are clear, which aren't, and what I'd change.
```

---

## Part 2 (10 min) — Polish your repo

Your GitHub is your résumé now. Make sure:

- [ ] Clear README with your name, live URL, screenshot
- [ ] All session journal entries present
- [ ] No hardcoded secrets (verify with Claude: `are there secrets in my code?`)
- [ ] CLAUDE.md present
- [ ] Commits with descriptive messages

If anything's missing, fix it now. It's your last chance before the demo.

---

## Part 3 (40 min) — Live demo to Federico

Federico joins or comes in person. You present.

**Demo rules**:
- Don't apologize for "how simple it is" — it's YOURS
- Speak with confidence even if your voice shakes (everyone's voice shakes)
- If Federico asks something you don't know, answer: "I don't know, but I'll research it"
- Afterwards, converse with him about the process
